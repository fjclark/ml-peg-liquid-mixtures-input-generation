# Decisions

The decisions behind the ml-peg `binary_mixtures` benchmark: 10 binary mixtures at 3 compositions (49 boxes with the pure liquids), built and run with Sage 2.3.0, scored by MAZE. The top section is the current state, one bullet per decision with its reason and evidence. The [history](#history) at the bottom lists every dated change, including those since superseded. Decisions were made by the benchmark author, many on Claude's recommendation, in planning sessions from 11 October to 9 November 2026.

The rules are implemented in `src/liquid_mixtures_inputs/` and listed in [selection/report.md](selection/report.md). Data problems found along the way are in [docs/data_issues.md](docs/data_issues.md).

## Current decisions

### Scope and data

- **Properties.** ΔHmix, mixture density and V^E of binary liquid mixtures, plus pure densities not already scored by ml-peg. ml-peg had no ΔHmix benchmark and no excess properties beyond the ethanol-water density curve.
- **Three compositions per system** (near x = 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75), even at about 1.7× the liquid_densities cost, because composition dependence is the point of ΔHmix.
- **Maximally informative systems.** Every system needs ΔHmix and density at matching compositions, pure densities, and experimental uncertainties throughout, so each box scores several properties.
- **No overlap with ml-peg.** The ethanol-water density curve, water density and the 63 liquid_densities liquids are not re-scored; their pure boxes are still simulated, as ΔHmix needs them.
- **Sources.** The ash-sage-rc2 (Sage 2.3 refit) training and validation sets are the main pool, because they carry experimental uncertainties; the Sage 2.0 set has none. Every source is pinned to a commit.
- **ThermoML archive screen.** The Sage sets have no S, carboxylic acid, imidazole, carbamate or usable N-H amide, so a one-off command (`screen-archive`) screens the NIST ThermoML archive snapshot ([10.18434/mds2-2422](https://doi.org/10.18434/mds2-2422), papers from about 2003 to 2019) for systems containing a wanted functional group, at 297 to 315 K, with every hard filter that needs no Sage results. It writes `selection/archive_candidates.csv`; a chosen system enters `selection.yml` through `extra_dois` with an `exception`, so the main selection never reads the archive.
- **Full isotherms from the source paper.** Isotherms and pure densities come from each mixture's own paper through the NIST ThermoML archive, and every pure density is cross-checked against ml-peg, Sage 2.0 and DIPPR/NIST (differences above 0.002 g/mL are flagged). The rc2 sets keep only 3 points per isotherm and include wrong pure densities (DMF, 1-propanol, piperidine) that flip the sign of V^E ([data_issues.md, 2.3](docs/data_issues.md#23-pure-densities-that-disagree-with-other-sources-verified-against-two-or-more-independent-papers)).
- **V^E uncertainties.** V^E is derived from the pure densities of the same paper. The density σ is treated as an error shared by every density in that paper (it cancels in V^E), and the paper's RMS residual about its Redlich-Kister fit gives the random part. Propagating the flat TRC σ as independent had made V^E error bars up to 40× the scatter (dipropylamine + DMF: 0.13 against 0.003 cm³/mol).
- **Methanol + water** densities from 10.1016/j.jct.2007.05.004 only, so its pure methanol is used for both V^E and the mixture densities; water is IAPWS-95.
- **Structure checks.** Every component must have zero formal charge, no enol or imidic acid motif, and match RDKit's canonical tautomer. The Sage data store 2-pyrrolidone as the wrong tautomer, and a wrong starting species cannot convert back in a 1 ns run (acetone's enol is ~10⁻⁸ of the equilibrium mixture, [Chiang et al. 1984](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00314a055)).

### Filters

36 of the 248 systems with enough data pass every filter ([funnel](selection/report.md#screening-funnel)).

- **Elements** H, C, N, O, F, S and Cl only. All are covered by MACE-OFF23 ([arXiv:2312.15211](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15211)), AIMNet2 and AceFF-2 ([arXiv:2601.00581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00581)). F and S were added on 2026-11-05 for functional-group coverage.
- **No acid-base pairs.** A carboxylic acid with an amine, imidazole or pyridine-type N would transfer protons and form ions; such pairs fail.
- **Size and dynamics.** ≤ 10 heavy atoms per molecule, and no viscous liquids (polyols, ethanolamines, polyamines), for cost and convergence in 1 ns of production.
- **No likely two-phase systems.** In a small box a model can stay mixed where the real system separates, or the reverse. Screens: H^E linear in x (the lever-rule signature, tested only when the largest |H^E| ≥ 0.4 kJ/mol, as ideal mixtures are also linear), H^E(x = 0.5) ≤ RT/2, and a literature miscibility check for every endothermic system.
- **Signal.** |ΔHmix(x ≈ 0.5)| ≥ 0.4 kJ/mol, well above the single-point detection limit.
- **Sage convergence.** Published Sage 3-replicate SD ≤ 0.10 kJ/mol (ΔHmix) and ≤ 0.001 g/mL (density), as a large spread suggests slow equilibration.
- **Uncertainties.** Relative ΔHmix uncertainty ≤ 15 % and pure-density uncertainties required, which removes placeholder errors (flat 25 %).
- **Cost budget.** Every box together must stay within 89,000 atoms (the 76,065 atoms of the 50-box set plus about 17 %: a 15 % budget, raised to fit N-methylacetamide + water and an ester); `select_systems.py` fails above it.

### Functional-group coverage

- **Groups as SMARTS** in `selection.yml` (`functional_groups`), chosen for biological importance: N-H amide (required), peptide-like secondary amide, ester, carboxylic acid, imidazole, carbamate, sulfur and fluorine. `select_systems.py` fails if a required group is neither in a curated system nor listed in `known_gaps` with its reason, and the report tabulates every system's groups (including the already covered ones in `other_groups`) and the cheapest passing candidates for each gap. A human picks; no optimiser.
- **Amides are acyclic**, so lactams (cis amides) do not count, and **N-methylformamide is not used**: amide C-N rotation is far slower than a 1 ns run, so its ~10 % cis fraction would stay at whatever the box was built with. N-methylacetamide, the standard peptide-bond model, is predominantly trans, so it is used with every molecule starting trans (`build_boxes.py` keeps only trans-amide conformers and restrains them trans during the build; the missing cis minority is a small, documented bias). The ThermoML archive has its ΔHmix only with aromatics at 303.15 K, without mixture densities ([10.1021/je700414c](https://doi.org/10.1021/je700414c)), so the water + N-methylacetamide ΔHmix at 50 °C of [de Haan and Gmehling (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1021/je950294h), which has no ThermoML record, is entered by hand in `manual_points.csv` (every point within 2 % of the paper's Redlich-Kister fit) and paired with the densities of [Makarov et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jct.2020.106233).
- **Acids only in an apolar solvent**, if at all; none passed (the only archive acid system, acetic acid + acetic anhydride, fails the signal floor).

### Systems

- **The 10 systems** are listed with their data in [selection/report.md](selection/report.md#selected-systems), 7 exothermic and 3 endothermic. On review (2026-10-14) the set was reshaped to have fewer alcohols, a non-polar π control (heptane + toluene), a wider ΔHmix range (NMP + 1,2-dichloroethane, -1.26 kJ/mol) and ~~O-H···π (1-propanol + benzene)~~ (dropped on 2026-11-09).
- **Aqueous systems.** Methanol + water (the smallest Sage spread in the pool, so it calibrates the noise) and water + pyridine at 303.15 K, ΔHmix only (the aromatic N-water motif of histidine and nucleobases; ThermoML mislabels the temperature, [data_issues.md, 2.2](docs/data_issues.md#22-pyridine--water-δhmix-temperature-mislabelled-verified)). Excluded: ethanol + water (already in ml-peg), 2-(dimethylamino)ethanol + water (large Sage SD), 2-pyrrolidone + water (viscous, wrong tautomer, placeholder errors), and acetophenone, acetylacetone and ethyl acetoacetate + water (likely two-phase).
- **Piperidine + water dropped.** Nitrogen inversion is frozen in Sage even at 500 K, so ΔHmix depended on the starting N-H orientation by ~0.3 kJ/mol.
- **NMP + 1,2-dichloroethane kept.** Its slow DCE torsion sampling is a Sage artefact: Sage's trans to gauche barrier is 25.4 kJ/mol against 17.3 kJ/mol for MACE-OFF23(M), which makes Sage's flips about 27× too slow ([Sage report](data/sage_summary/report.md#nmp--12-dichloroethane)). A model with a reference-like barrier relaxes in about 40 ps, inside the equilibration. ΔHmix comes from 10.1016/j.jct.2008.01.024 only, at its points nearest x = 0.2, 0.5 and 0.8, as the two papers disagree away from equimolar.
- **Three systems dropped for cost** (13 to 10 systems, 64 to 50 boxes, about 190 to 140 GPU-days per model). DMAE + methanol: MLP runs would start from Sage's conformer populations, which could hide a model that favours the wrong conformer; it had the largest gaps from the published 1000-molecule Sage runs (ΔHmix -0.35 kJ/mol, z = -3.6, at x = 0.75); and its association partly overlaps methanol + water and 1-propanol + benzene. Methyl pentanoate + octane and 2-butanone + dibutylamine: the two most expensive systems (11 % and 10 % of the atoms, from 2,000 to 2,800-atom pure boxes); the first overlaps propionitrile + tert-amyl ethyl ether (dipoles diluted by a weak partner), and the second has the weakest signal (+0.42 to +0.62 kJ/mol). The resolvable ΔHmix MAE difference barely changes (0.096 to 0.094 kJ/mol) ([power analysis](data/power_analysis/report.md)). Water + pyridine was kept despite sharing pyridine with pyrrole + pyridine: its O-H···N donor, water network and microheterogeneity are not tested elsewhere.
- **Functional-group additions (2026-11-05)**, within the budget (88,640 atoms, +16.5 %):
  - **1-Methylimidazole + ethanol** (−1.00 kJ/mol, [10.1016/j.jct.2018.12.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jct.2018.12.019)) replaces tetrahydropyran + ethanol at about the same cost and covers the imidazole ring. It has no N-H, so histidine's N-H tautomerism is not covered. The sample was 99 % pure and is hygroscopic; the paper records no water content.
  - **N-methylacetamide + water at 323.15 K** (−1.61 kJ/mol near equimolar, down to −1.71 kJ/mol) covers the peptide-like secondary amide and amide hydration, the first amide-water system. N-methylacetamide melts near 301 K, so it runs at 50 °C, with its own pure N-methylacetamide and water boxes at 323.15 K (water from IAPWS-95). It replaced formamide + 1-propanol (a primary amide like the asparagine and glutamine side chains, +0.74 kJ/mol), which was added earlier the same day and is now a reserve; keeping both would have cost 93,887 atoms.
  - **Ethyl acetate + ethanol** (+1.21 kJ/mol; ΔHmix from [10.1021/je200595b](https://doi.org/10.1021/je200595b), densities and both pure liquids from [10.1016/j.jct.2007.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jct.2007.05.004)) covers the ester, with the most common ester (as in lipid esters and acetylcholine), and shares the ethanol box. Its largest published Sage repeat SD is 0.12 kJ/mol (limit 0.10), so it is kept as an exception. Our 5 replicates have SDs of 0.13, 0.04 and 0.07 kJ/mol (pooled 0.088 kJ/mol, below the 0.091 kJ/mol median floor of σ_Sage); 13 of the 36 ΔHmix points in our smaller boxes exceed 0.10 kJ/mol, so it is not unusually noisy and stays. Ethyl acetate + water would be more biological but is only partially miscible; the only ester + water system with data, ethyl acetoacetate + water, is two-phase.
  - **2,2,2-Trifluoroethanol + methanol** (−0.85 kJ/mol) was added for fluorine and then replaced by the ester, which was judged more important; both did not fit the budget. It is a reserve, as is THF + TFE (−1.96 kJ/mol).
  - The new systems have no published Sage repeat SDs; our own Sage replicates replace them, as for water + pyridine.
- **Two systems dropped for diversity (2026-11-09)** (12 to 10 systems, 59 to 49 boxes, 88,640 to 73,031 atoms, about 161 to 133 GPU-days per model), to have fewer alcohols and better reflect biomolecular groups. 1-Propanol + benzene: its alcohol dilution (+0.97 kJ/mol) repeats ethyl acetate + ethanol (+1.21 kJ/mol), π systems are still covered by heptane + toluene and the two pyridine systems, and it was the noisiest Sage reference (replicate SDs 0.17 to 0.27 kJ/mol) and the most often flagged in the Sage inspection; only O-H···π is lost. Propionitrile + tert-amyl ethyl ether: nitriles and branched ethers are rare in biomolecules, its endothermic dipolar dilution (+0.70 kJ/mol) and Sage error repeat dipropylamine + DMF, and it was the second most expensive system (8,697 atoms with its pure boxes). That leaves three alcohol systems (methanol + water, 1-methylimidazole + ethanol, ethyl acetate + ethanol), each with a distinct role. Water + pyridine was considered for dropping instead (no mixture densities, four exceptions) but kept: it is the only aromatic-water system and one of the two where Sage gets the sign of ΔHmix wrong, with low noise. THF + 2,2,2-trifluoroethanol (-1.96 kJ/mol, [10.1021/je034076x](https://doi.org/10.1021/je034076x)), which would cover the ether and fluorine, is a reserve.
- **Known gaps**, stated in the report from `known_gaps` in `selection.yml`: no fluorine (2,2,2-trifluoroethanol + methanol passes but did not fit the budget), no ether (propionitrile + tert-amyl ethyl ether, dropped on 2026-11-09, had no hydrogen-bond donor, so never tested the ether as an acceptor; THF + TFE is the reserve), carboxylic acid, carbamate or sulfur (1-propanol + DMSO passes but did not fit), and the set reflects ThermoML and Sage coverage. Reserves are kept in `selection.yml`.

### Boxes and protocol

- **Box size.** Edge ≥ 25 Å, ≥ 100 molecules and ≥ 20 of the minority component (952 to 2,300 atoms). Half of a 21.5 Å box is below the ~12 Å receptive field of a 2-layer, 6 Å MLP, precision per GPU-hour does not depend on box size, and 1000-molecule boxes would cost 10 to 15× more. Sage results in our boxes match the published 1000-molecule runs ([Sage report](data/sage_summary/report.md#box-size)).
- **Box build.** Packed at the experimental density with Sage 2.3.0, each molecule starting from the lowest-MMFF of 10 ETKDG conformers, annealed (450 K cooled to T over 1.6 ns) and equilibrated for 500 ps NVT. The first Sage runs showed slow conformer equilibria (tetrahydropyran, DCE, DMAE) and dependence on the starting conformer. Acyclic secondary amides (N-methylacetamide) start trans: only conformers with every C-C(=O)-N-C dihedral beyond 150° are considered, and a 100 kJ/mol torsion restraint holds every amide trans during the build only (packing, anneal and NVT equilibration). Without it, about 5 % of N-methylacetamide molecules turned cis during the 450 K anneal in Sage 2.3.0 (3 to 6 per box), so Sage's amide rotation is easier than the 1 ns timescale argument assumes. The restraint is removed before every run; in the unrestrained Sage runs at 323.15 K at most 3 of 115 N-methylacetamide molecules (2.6 %) are cis after 2.2 ns, and most boxes have none.
- **Protocol.** One run per model and box: 0.2 ns NPT equilibration and 1 ns production, for cost: 0.2 + 2 ns would cost 1.8× as much (about 133 against 244 MACE-OFF23(M) A100 GPU-days per model) and only narrow the resolvable ΔHmix MAE difference from 0.083 to 0.067 kJ/mol, while 1 ns already resolves density gaps far smaller than those between published MLPs. Equilibration stays at 0.2 ns, as the 100 to 200 ps window is still shifted for Sage ([power analysis](data/power_analysis/report.md)).
- **One estimator** for Sage and the MLPs: ml-peg's `timeseries.py` (autocorrelation) on every logged sample, without binning.

### Sage reference

- **Sage 2.3.0 throughout**, 5 replicates per box: replicate 0 is the distributed box, replicates 1 to 4 are independently re-packed. Each run is 2.2 ns, so the power analysis can compare the protocol with 2 ns of production, but every Sage summary (σ_Sage, the reference values and Sage's scores) uses only the protocol's window, 0.2 to 1.2 ns. The replicate spread gives σ_Sage. Mixing versions would mix parameters: 2.3.0 liquids are 0.01 to 0.03 g/mL denser than 2.2.1.
- **Finite-size check** against the published `fb-fit-v3-single-mean-k100` runs (2.3.0rc2, parameters identical to the released 2.3.0; only the NAGL model differs).
- **Significance.** Family-wise 0.05 (`FLAG_P`), Holm-adjusted for per-box tests and Bonferroni over the pooled tests.

### Metrics and thresholds

- **MAZE for every property** (ΔHmix, mixture density, V^E and pure density), with the MAE shown at weight 0. V^E uses MAE, not RMSE.
- **Denominator.** σ_ref = sqrt(σ_Sage² + σ_exp²). σ_Sage is the replicate SD pooled (RMS) over a system's compositions and floored at the median over systems, so that no system dominates MAZE because Sage happens to sample it quietly. NMP + DCE and pure DCE are set to the floor on all four properties, as their spread comes from Sage's slow torsion.
- **Thresholds from the error analysis.** 'Good' is the score of a model that matches experiment exactly, with one 0.2 + 1 ns run per box, plus 2 SD of its noise. 'Bad' is 5× Sage's score, as Sage was trained on these properties. Mixture and pure densities share one set of thresholds for simplicity, the larger of their two values. Values in [thresholds.csv](data/power_analysis/thresholds.csv), ready to paste as [metrics_snippet.yml](data/power_analysis/metrics_snippet.yml). They must be recomputed if the protocol changes.
